# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI Data Analytics Project

### DataX Lab Internship — Task 3: Dashboard Design

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to provide business stakeholders with a clear and reliable overview of sales performance across **products, countries, deal sizes, and order statuses**.

The project focuses on transforming raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPIs, filters, and trend analysis.



## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to design an interactive sales dashboard that enables stakeholders to:

* Monitor overall sales performance at a glance
* Track monthly and yearly sales trends
* Identify top-performing product lines
* Analyze sales performance by country and market
* Understand deal-size distribution
* Monitor order status and sales contribution
* Explore different business segments through interactive filters
* Support data-driven business decision-making

**Internship:** DataX Lab Internship
**Task:** Task 3 — Dashboard Design
**Tool:** Power BI
**Dataset:** Sales / Financial Dataset (Kaggle-style sales data)

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. KPI Cards

The dashboard provides a high-level overview through KPI cards displaying:

* Total Sales
* Total Units Sold
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value
* YoY Sales Growth

These KPIs allow stakeholders to understand overall business performance quickly.



### 2. Sales Trend Analysis

The dashboard includes monthly and yearly sales trend analysis covering **2003–2005**.

Key observation:

* Sales show clear growth from **2003 to 2004**
* **2005 contains partial-year data through May**, so it should not be directly compared with complete years without considering data coverage



### 3. Product & Market Performance

#### Sales by Product Line

**Classic Cars** is the highest-performing product line, generating approximately:

> **$3.92M in sales**

This indicates that Classic Cars represents a major contributor to overall revenue.

#### Sales by Country

**USA** is the largest market by sales, generating approximately:

> **$3.63M in sales**

This highlights the importance of the USA market within the dataset.



### 4. Sales Mix & Order Status

#### Sales by Deal Size

**Medium-sized deals** contribute the largest share of sales, generating approximately:

> **$6.09M**

This indicates that medium-sized transactions are the primary contributor to sales revenue.

#### Sales by Order Status

The majority of sales revenue comes from **Shipped** orders, contributing approximately:

> **$9.29M**

This provides a useful view of sales distribution across order statuses.



## 🎛️ Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive slicers and cross-filtering capabilities.

Available filters include:

* **Year**
* **Country**
* **Product Line**
* **Deal Size**
* **Order Status**

Users can combine multiple filters to explore specific business segments and investigate different sales patterns.

### Cross-Filtering

Selecting a data point in one visual dynamically filters related visuals, allowing users to explore relationships between different dimensions of the dataset.

## 🎨 Design Guidelines Followed

The dashboard was designed following common business intelligence and data visualization principles:

* Relevant KPIs selected for quick performance monitoring
* Interactive slicers for user-driven analysis
* Monthly and yearly time-series analysis
* Appropriate chart selection for different analytical questions
* Summary cards for high-level metrics

* Clear visual hierarchy
* Minimal visual clutter
* Business insights highlighted alongside visualizations
* Interactive cross-filtering between visuals



## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the available slicers:

   * Year
   * Country
   * Product Line
   * Deal Size
   * Order Status
4. Select individual data points in charts to activate cross-filtering.
5. Review KPI cards for high-level performance.
6. Explore trend, product, market, deal-size, and order-status visualizations.
